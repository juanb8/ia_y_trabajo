# Research Questions

## Technology and software layoffs: AI impact or broader economic trends?

- What has happened to layoffs and employment in software and related
technology jobs?

Employers explicitly linked layoffs to AI in 34 of 243 events (14%)
and cited AI alongside other factors in 148 (61%);
these figures show stated explanations, not proven causes.
(AI Layoffs, *Counting AI Layoffs from the Employer’s Own Words*, 2026.)

- To what extent are these changes attributable to AI,
rather than broader economic or industry trends?

- Do employers' stated AI-related reasons for layoffs align with available evidence?

## New jobs supporting AI

- What kinds of work, such as data annotation, evaluation, and moderation,
are growing around AI?
- How do their pay, security, benefits, and working conditions compare with
software engineering jobs?

## Human labor behind AI training

- What human labor and creative or professional work contribute to training AI models?
- How is that contribution recognized or compensated?

## Food for thought: how might these trends connect?

Taken together, do the findings suggest that AI is not only changing or replacing some work,
but also shifting work and risk between workers while relying on human contributions
that may go uncompensated?
